M1-2/R6A zoning questions

How built-out are M1-2/R6A lots?

The average M1-2/R6A lot is built to 2.43 FAR against an average maximum residential FAR of 3 in MapPLUTO 26v2, so about 19% of allowed floor area is unused on the average lot. Lots vary; open any one on the map.
