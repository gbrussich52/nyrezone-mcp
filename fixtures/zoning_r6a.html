R6A Zoning in NYC

R6A is a mid-density apartment zoning district in New York City. It allows a residential FAR of 3.0 and buildings up to 75 ft tall. 15,188 tax lots carry the R6A designation, 59% of them in Brooklyn. Their average built FAR is 2.02, against 3 allowed.

R6A zoning questions

What is the maximum FAR in R6A?

Residential FAR in R6A is 3.0 (ZR 23-22). Floor area on a lot is that ratio times the lot area.

How tall can a building be in R6A?

R6A caps buildings at 75 feet, with a base of 40 to 65 ft (ZR 23-432). With the affordable-housing bonus the cap is 95 feet.

How built-out are R6A lots?

The average R6A lot is built to 2.02 FAR against an average maximum residential FAR of 3 in MapPLUTO 26v2, so about 33% of allowed floor area is unused on the average lot. Lots vary; open any one on the map.

Does City of Yes affect R6A lots?

15,188 of 15,188 R6A lots (100%) flag as touched by at least one City of Yes for Housing Opportunity program in NYRezone's screening. Eligibility is lot by lot; verify each one.
